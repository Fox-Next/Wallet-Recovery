This public repository contains documentation only. It does not contain the Wallet Recovery application, its source code or recovery engine, executable packages, activation services, activation-code generators, signing keys, API credentials, or license secrets. Official downloads are available from the official website (`foxnext.net`) and APKPure ([Wallet Recovery](https://apkpure.com/p/net.foxnext.ru.walletrecovery)); this repository does not host download files.

Wallet Recovery is intended to help a rightful owner recover access to their own wallet when they have forgotten some BIP-39 recovery words or their order. Current Windows and Android versions support English, Persian, Russian, and Simplified Chinese. The application supports BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, and Tron.

## English

### Quick start
1. Open the official Wallet Recovery product page or the localized download page below (`https://foxnext.net`). The Android version is also published on APKPure, where you can download [Wallet Recovery](https://apkpure.com/p/net.foxnext.ru.walletrecovery).
2. Install and launch the version for your device. English is the default; choose another supported language in the app when needed.
3. Use the search only for a wallet you own or are explicitly authorized to recover. Enter the phrase length, place the words you remember in their known positions, and mark unknown positions as the app instructs. Add your own wallet's public target address and confirm its derivation path and account/index range from your wallet documentation or backup.
4. Select only the networks you need and start the search. Review the result and the output location shown in the app's Guide.
5. Never send your recovery phrase, passphrase, private key, or recovery results to support or publish them online.

### Privacy and security
Recovery phrases, passphrases, and private keys are processed on your device and are not sent to Fox Next servers by the recovery workflow. If you enable network checks, the app sends derived **public addresses** to the RPC or explorer provider you selected; that provider handles those requests under its own privacy policy. The website activation flow submits the Device ID and transaction ID (TX ID) to Fox Next for payment and activation verification. Do not put wallet secrets in the activation form.

### How to verify the download
Use the HTTPS site and confirm the domain is exactly `foxnext.net`. Follow the Wallet Recovery page to its localized download page. If the site publishes a file signature or checksum, compare it before running the file; do not assume one is available. You may also scan the downloaded file with reputable security software. This is a closed-source product: this public documentation repository does not let you independently inspect or audit the application code, and an official-site link is not a claim of an independent security audit. Avoid copies, repackaged files, and download links sent in chats.

### Ethical use and responsibility
Use Wallet Recovery only for your own wallet or with the owner's explicit permission. Do not use it to access another person's wallet or obtain, disclose, or misuse another person's recovery phrase or keys. You are responsible for authorization, compliance with applicable laws, and protecting your information. Fox Next promotes ethical use and respect for privacy and ownership.

### Activation and payment methods
Open the [official activation page](https://foxnext.net/en/wallet-recovery-activation.html) and choose your preferred payment method (single-device or multi-device packages for Windows and Android):
- **Bank cards (`Visa / Mastercard / Link`):** Secure online checkout with instant activation code delivery.
- **Digital wallets (`Google Pay / Apple Pay`):** Available based on your region and device via secure checkout.
- **Cryptocurrency (`USDT` on `BNB Smart Chain · BEP-20`):** Transfer `BSC-USD` and submit the transaction hash (`TX Hash`) for automated verification.
- **Direct support & other crypto networks:** For additional cryptocurrency networks or direct assistance, contact Telegram support ([@HiFoxnext](https://t.me/HiFoxnext)) or email (`support@foxnext.net`) with your `Device ID`.
- **How activation works:** Paste the received activation code into Wallet Recovery to bind a permanent license to your `Device ID`. Recovery phrases, passphrases, and private keys are never sent.
